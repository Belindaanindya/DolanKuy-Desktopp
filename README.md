# DolanKuy

## Project Overview

**DolanKuy** is a tourism reference application in Indonesia, designed across **three platforms**:

* **Mobile (Android)** → for end-users to browse destinations and accommodations
* **Website** → for end-users to view tourism catalogs
* **Desktop (Admin Panel)** → for administrators to manage tourism data

The project aims to simplify holiday planning by providing accurate, accessible information about tourist destinations and nearby accommodations, helping both **travelers** and **business owners** in the tourism sector.

## Business Understanding

Tourism is one of the biggest contributors to regional income and has a significant multiplier effect on the economy. However, many tourist destinations in Indonesia remain less known, making holiday planning difficult for travelers.

**Problem Statement:**

* Difficulty in planning holidays due to scattered and incomplete information
* Lack of visibility for local tourism businesses

**Solution with DolanKuy:**

* Provides a **centralized platform** for tourism information
* Features **maps, distance calculation, and accommodations** around destinations
* Allows **business owners** to promote their services (restaurants, souvenir shops, etc.)

**Benefits:**

* Travelers: Access to complete and reliable tourism info for better planning
* Business Owners: A marketing platform to reach more customers

---

## Project Scope / Data Understanding

Data collected from **Google** and **Google Maps**, including:

* Tourist destinations
* Accommodations (restaurants, hotels, souvenir shops, etc.)
